# Preparation reference review

Jayasri supplied two YouTube Shorts while reviewing the concept:

- [Andy Cooks — I Made a Cup of Anaerobic Coffee from scratch](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/t6WeVk7tJXU): observed harvesting, processing/drying stages, and beans being handled before grinding. This is a particular processing example, not a claim that every coffee follows an anaerobic process.
- [Ali Abdaal — The BEST Way to Make Coffee](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/e108RLHXi6E): observed weighing beans, a Clever Dripper with filter paper, adding coffee and water, stirring, and draining into a mug. This is filter brewing, not the espresso recipe used in the latte concept.

Both were reviewed in the browser. Their footage is not downloaded, copied, or embedded in this project. The new grinder plate is separately generated concept artwork.

## Changes prompted by the review

- Replace the oversized animated beans on a plate with a grinder: whole beans stay in the hopper, and ground coffee is shown in the portafilter.
- Remove the falling-lid effect. It previously cropped the top of a completed-drink plate and accidentally carried coffee with it. The final phase now holds the completed open cup for a pickup check; the pickup product image may show its fitted lid.
- Move the liquid fill and surface close to the open rim, leaving a small gap instead of a large empty section. This applies to regular, light, and no ice.
- Keep the requested sequence: ice, espresso, cold milk, final check, automatic pickup.

The sequence remains a one-minute simulation. It is not live preparation footage or verified café operating instructions.

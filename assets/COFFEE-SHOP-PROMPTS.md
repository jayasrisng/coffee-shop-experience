# Coffee Shop concept artwork

Created using built-in image generation. Photorealistic generated imagery, not photographs of a real café’s products. The logo and matching decal are original native SVG artwork: a rising sun above a coffee cup in yellow and brown.

## cups

Use case: product-mockup. Create an exact 2 by 2 grid of four premium realistic coffee product photographs, square canvas. Each equal square quadrant has exactly ONE complete centered cup at the same scale, with generous margin all around, cup occupying 70 percent of each tile height, top and bottom entirely within that quadrant. NO grid borders. All backgrounds are uniform warm pale vanilla #F5EBD8, soft natural studio shadows. TOP LEFT: iced latte in clear disposable cafe cup, transparent flat lid, real ice, rich espresso mixing with milk. TOP RIGHT: iced americano dark espresso over translucent ice, clear cup and lid. BOTTOM LEFT: iced mocha, chocolate milk coffee, clear cup, whipped cream and little cocoa dust. BOTTOM RIGHT: iced matcha latte, beautiful authentic green matcha milk with ice, clear cup and flat lid. Each cup has a small plain buttery yellow round sticker at front center with absolutely NO symbol or text. Realistic plastic rim, condensation, beautiful cafe editorial lighting, shot from slightly above front, 85mm product photography, no straws, no text, no brand names, no logos, no watermarks. Not illustration, not vector. Consistent scale and background across all four exact quadrants. This will be used as a CSS sprite.

## latte

Use case: product-mockup. One single ultra photorealistic iced latte in a clear takeaway coffee cup with clear flat sip lid, isolated on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background, no checkerboard. Full cup centered, upright front view very slightly from above, portrait 4:5 canvas. Cup occupies 83 percent height with generous transparent margins. Premium real product photograph: rich caramel espresso and ivory milk, organic subtle gradients not drawn ribbons, clear chunky ice visible at top, finely detailed transparent plastic walls, natural tiny condensation droplets, beautiful specular light. Small blank butter-yellow round sticker at front center of cup at about 62 percent from top, completely blank with no symbols. No straw. No text, no logos, no watermark, no table, no backdrop. Realistic cutout for website menu and final pickup. Warm soft studio lighting, very sharp cup details. Not cartoon or vector.

## no-ice

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit this isolated photorealistic latte cup. Preserve exactly same transparent background, same centered upright cup position, proportions, camera angle, clear lid/rim, blank yellow round sticker and lighting. Change ONLY the drink to NO ICE: remove all visible ice cubes, fill with a smooth cold caramel latte, natural subtle milk/espresso marbling but no ice shapes. Genuinely transparent background. No text, no logos, no extra objects. Photorealistic clear plastic takeaway cup.
